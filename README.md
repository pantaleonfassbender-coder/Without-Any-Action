# Without Any Action

*Operation Pastorius, Long Island and Florida, June 1942 – four versions of how eight saboteurs were caught.*

In June 1942 two German U-boats landed four saboteurs each: at Amagansett on Long Island (12/13 June) and at Ponte Vedra Beach, Florida (16/17 June). The FBI announced on 27 June that it had caught all eight. The record shows another sequence. One of the men had telephoned the FBI's New York office on 14 June. A memo written that night was "prepared only for the purpose of recording the call". In Florida nobody saw the landing.

A free browser game on the American side, in three scenarios:

- **I. The Beach:** the Coast Guard station at Amagansett, then the naval section base at Mayport.
- **II. The Call:** the FBI, 14–27 June.
- **III. The Credit:** what the country is told, 27 June – 8 August.

Nobody fights in this game. The commission's verdicts and the executions are fixed record, never a move.

## Files

- `index.html`, `play.html`, `guide.html`, `plates.html`: the pages.
- `data-wa.js`: the record (with source marks) and the game data.
- `engine.js`: the rules, without the DOM. `node mc.js` runs a bench.
- `game.js`, `reckoning.js`, `plates.js`: the interface.
- `tools/make-assets.py`: builds the board and plates from `../quellen`.
- `itch/`: the package (`python itch/build-itch.py`), cover and screenshots.
